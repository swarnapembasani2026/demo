PLAY FAIR. PLAY BOLD.

Good game
starts here.

Big swings are encouraged. Respect for people, privacy, and the rules is non-negotiable.

I can play by those rules ↗
01

Build with your team

Submit work created by your registered team during the event. Credit collaborators and disclose any third-party tools or code you use.

02

Respect the boundaries

Use only systems and data you are authorized to access. Never test security on real services, accounts, or personal data.

03

Keep it kind

Make room for different voices. Harassment, discrimination, or unsafe conduct is not part of the game; organizers may remove participants who violate the code of conduct.

04

Make your demo accessible

Explain what your project does, who it helps, and what you built. Be honest about limitations and what still needs work.
